Starter Modern House

Learn how to build a sleek, resource-friendly modern home for your early game survival world.

Category: Modern Difficulty: Easy Time: 15 Mins

Required Materials

  • 64x Smooth Quartz Blocks
  • 32x White Concrete
  • 48x Glass Panes
  • 20x Dark Oak Planks
Step 1

Laying the Foundation

Mark out a 9x9 square using smooth quartz blocks. Dig out the interior floor and replace it with dark oak planks to create a warm contrast against the white walls.

[ Image: Foundation Layout ]
Step 2

Building the Walls & Pillars

Raise the corners using white concrete pillars 4 blocks high. Fill in the front and side walls with smooth quartz, leaving space for large glass window panes.

[ Image: Wall Framing ]
Step 3

Roof & Glass Installation

Cap off the top with a flat quartz roof, extending it out by one block to create a clean overhang. Install glass panes into the window frames and add door fixtures.

[ Image: Finished Roof & Windows ]
